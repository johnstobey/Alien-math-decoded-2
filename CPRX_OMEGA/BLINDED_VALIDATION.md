# Held-out radial test

Frozen training data before evaluating final annuli: bands 0–1; bands 2–5 not used for offset selection. The holdout is independent data but not truly blinded from the analyst who selected overall feature class and center.

Trained shift 2, held-out mean 0.121309; exact null of 12 competing shifts 0.4167. This is **not a match against matched procedural-image null models**. Consequently no general correspondence claim is accepted.
