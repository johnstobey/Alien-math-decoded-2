# Refutations and limitations

- Negative controls: `A(0)=5` false (actual 6); `rho(0)=0` false (actual 11); 12-ring reflection distance quotient has 7, not 6 classes; intersection with empty set remains empty.
- Equal-size sector labels alone are not a geometric isomorphism: a synthetic 12-bin target can represent D12 exactly even if raw pixels are random. Thus a perfect formal commuting diagram on **constructed labels** does not prove physical correspondence.
- The upper-left spiral-chain fits and brightness rotational MSE in earlier files are not source-to-vortex witnesses.
